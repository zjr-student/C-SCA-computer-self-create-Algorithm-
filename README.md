# C-SCA: Autonomous Mathematical Structure Discovery Engine

---

## One-Line Summary

**Let AI discover new mathematical algorithms on its own.**

---

## What Does It Do?

Today's AI algorithms (Transformer, backpropagation, etc.) are all designed by humans. C-SCA lets AI explore the mathematical space to **find new algorithms** on its own—structures that humans may have never seen before.

---

## How It Works

1. **MAI Encoding**: Translate mathematical concepts into numbers (e.g., "integral" → 10)
2. **C-SCA Engine**: Randomly combine, validate, and evolve structures in the numeric space
3. **Distributed Compute**: Leverage global GPU resources to search in parallel

---

## Why It Matters

- Human-discovered algorithms are only a tiny fraction of the total mathematical space
- C-SCA explores the **entire mathematical space**
- It may discover better architectures than Transformer, and better learning rules than backpropagation
